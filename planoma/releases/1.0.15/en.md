# What's New in Version 1.0.15

## New Features

### Your Whole Day on One Screen
Habits and activities used to live in two separate tabs. Now they can share a single Today screen, so everything you are working on is in one place. If you preferred them apart, that is still an option in Settings.

- A card at the top shows how your week is going: how much you have done, which goals you have closed, and how many streaks you have running.
- The card tucks itself away as soon as you start scrolling, so it never gets in the way.

### Planoma on iPad
- On iPad you now get a proper sidebar instead of the small tab bar from the iPhone, so there is more room for your habits.

### See How Your Month Went
- Reports has a new Monthly view: how each habit did over the month, and you can flip back through earlier months.

### Calendars That Explain Themselves
- Every calendar now has a legend beneath it, so a colour or a pattern is never a guess: goal met, partial progress, covered by the period, no check-in, and — for the habits you are cutting down — within the limit or limit exceeded.
- A habit you only have to do twice a week no longer looks like five failures. Once you have hit the goal, the rest of the period is marked as covered, and it looks the same everywhere the calendar turns up: the habit's own screen, the reports, the year grid, and the widgets.
- Check-ins you recorded before the day you created a habit now appear on its calendar instead of quietly going missing.

### Share What You Have Built
- When you are on a good streak or you have had a strong week, Planoma offers to turn it into a picture you can share. One tap and it is ready.

### Check In Without Opening the App
- There is a new Quick Check-in button you can add to Control Center, your Lock Screen, or the Action button, set to whichever habit you like.
- The multi-habit widget has been redrawn as a row of tiles, so you can see and tick off several habits without leaving the Home Screen.

### Make It Feel Like Yours
- Three new color themes — Lagoon, Coral, and Golden — each with a matching app icon.
- Would you rather not be cheered on? You can now switch the coach off completely and keep just your progress and your buttons.
- Curious how a coach sounds before you pick one? You can read a sample first.
- Planoma greets you by name, and the greeting changes through the day. Your name is now editable, or removable, in Settings.

### Forgot to Check In Yesterday?
- You can now tick off, or untick, any past day from the weekly view. A day you simply forgot to open the app no longer has to stay marked as missed.

### Tidy Up in One Go
- Pick several habits at once and send them all to the trash together, instead of deleting them one at a time. You have 30 days to change your mind, in Settings → Recently Deleted.

### Getting Back on Track
- When more than one habit has been sitting untouched for a while, the "It's been a while" reminder now lets you choose which one to pick back up.

## Improvements

### Reports
- The weekly report is easier to read, and you can sort it by name, by what is going best, or by what needs your attention.
- The summary now says which days it is talking about, so a partial week is not mistaken for a bad one.
- Habits you are trying to do less of are now measured properly, and doing more than you planned no longer distorts the numbers.
- Timer habits are scored on the time you actually recorded.
- The monthly view has the same legend as the weekly one, and its calendar matches the rest of the app.

### Timers
- If a timer is already running and you start another one, Planoma now tells you which habit has it and for how long, and asks before switching. It used to switch without a word.
- A habit's own screen tells you when a timer is running elsewhere, before you tap anything.
- The elapsed time keeps counting correctly on a timer you have been running for more than a week.

### Everyday Use
- Opening a habit is much faster, and its screen no longer stutters while you scroll through it.
- Calendars fade into place instead of flashing an empty placeholder first.
- Habit cards glide between "to do" and "done" instead of jumping.
- Milestone celebrations now arrive one at a time, and a burst of them is gathered into a single card instead of a queue you have to tap through.
- Undoing a check-in works the same way everywhere in the app, and the undo button no longer shows up on a check-in that came from Apple Health, where it never did anything good.
- Deleting a habit confirms it with a short message, in your language.
- Your first day of the week is now respected everywhere, including the widgets.
- Your lists appear as soon as your data is ready, with no waiting on a loading screen that has nothing left to load.
- Scrolling on the main screen is smoother.
- Siri works straight away, with nothing to set up first.

### In Your Language
- Fixed copy that was showing up in Italian, or as a raw label, in German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian, and Chinese — on the daily progress card and in a handful of other places.

### Sync, Widgets, and Apple Watch
- The iCloud badge that floated over the whole screen is gone. In its place, a small icon in the list header appears only when a sync is genuinely taking a while, or when something has come up that only you can fix — signed out, out of iCloud storage. Tap it and you get the details.
- The list no longer jumps around while iCloud is syncing in the background.
- If iCloud sync ever misbehaves, Settings now has a Diagnostics section with a report you can copy and send us.
- The multi-habit widget shows the right state for each habit.
- Apple Watch cards look better and keep up with your iPhone more reliably.

### Speed and Stability
- Planoma uses a good deal less memory than it did, so your iPhone should stop closing it in the background as often as it was.
- Statistics are calculated in a fraction of the time, which is most of why the app feels quicker throughout.

## Fixes

- Emptying the trash no longer closes the app, and it can no longer take a habit you are still using with it.
- A timer you had running is no longer thrown away when you start a different one. The time you had clocked up gets saved.
- Past days in the weekly view were showing today's Apple Health and timer numbers. They now show what actually happened on the day.
- Apple Health progress no longer goes missing on days you were active more than once, no longer records the same check-in twice, and several health habits can now sync at the same time without closing the app.
- Your trophies survive turning iCloud sync on or off, and they no longer hang around after the habit they belonged to is gone for good.
- Reminders are no longer cancelled behind your back, and they survive a change to your sync settings.
- Your history is no longer lost if you go through the welcome screens again or restore an older backup.
- Habits no longer turn up twice after importing or restoring your data, and duplicates that an earlier sync left behind are repaired.
- An edit you made is no longer lost when switching iCloud sync on or off does not go through.
- The calendar widget starts updating again after you restart your iPhone.
- Apple Health syncing no longer runs over and over in the background.
- A habit card no longer falls back to an old count right after you check in.
- A habit you only do once per period can no longer be checked in twice.
- Your consistency score can reach 10 again, and the "Habits today" badge counts what is actually in the list beneath it.
- Sounds play to the end instead of being cut short.
- The notification that tells you a timer has finished now names the habit.
- Text no longer spills out of the layout on a few of the welcome screens.
- Fixed a handful of small errors in how streaks and statistics were counted.

Thank you for using Planoma! We are constantly working to make your habit tracking better.
