# What’s New in Version 1.0.19

- Redesigned habit details bring progress, target history and statistics together in a clearer layout, with paginated charts and an adaptive iPad layout.
- Move between days from Home and see weekly progress that stays consistent with the Today widget.
- Track water and caffeine with configurable quantities and units, with optional Apple Health synchronization.
- Start timers from the Play button, record completed sessions manually, and hold the + button to add a check-in for a specific date.
- Share your progress with a redesigned export screen.
- Clearer Apple Health permission guidance helps you restore synchronization while continuing to record check-ins in Planoma.
- Editing follows the habit’s color as you change it, and Apple Watch shows Health progress with the appropriate units.
