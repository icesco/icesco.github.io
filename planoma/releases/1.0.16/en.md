# What's New in Version 1.0.16

## New Experiences

- Creating a habit feels more natural: describe what you want to do, review Planoma's proposal, and customize it in a new live-preview screen. You can also freely browse every ready-made idea.
- Meet Ettore, Planoma's new companion. He appears at the right moments with a playful, motivating, or empathetic personality, and comes with two new app icons.
- Siri and Shortcuts can now show habit status, add or undo a check-in, open a habit, and start or stop timers. Your habits also appear in Spotlight search and follow you between devices with Handoff.
- The timer Live Activity has been redesigned across iPhone and Apple Watch, with clearer progress and controls that work without opening the app. Timers can now start from widgets too.

## Improvements

- Active streaks now highlight your best results on a podium, while the streak detail screen has a new navigable calendar and livelier animations.
- A running timer now uses the new native accessory above the tab bar, making it more compact and at home on iOS.
- The icon picker has been redesigned with search, clearer categories, and a much wider choice of emoji and symbols.
- The widget guide now walks you through the setup on a single iPhone, with simpler instructions that are easier to follow.

## Fixes

- Timer buttons in widgets now start the right timer, Siri recognizes spoken habit names more reliably even when the app is closed, and compact Dynamic Island timers render correctly.
- Habit edits and reminders are preserved more reliably through syncs, imports, and storage changes. On devices without Apple Intelligence, habit creation now opens directly in manual mode.

Thank you for using Planoma!
