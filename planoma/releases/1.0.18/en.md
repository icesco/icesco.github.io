# What's New in Version 1.0.18

## New

- Make the Planoma Today widget your own: choose sections, highlight important habits, apply filters, and switch pages right from the widget.
- Ettore now appears on Home with short messages that reflect your progress throughout the day.
- Personalize Planoma with six new themes and new alternate app icons, including one featuring Ettore.
- Planoma is now available in Japanese.

## Improvements

- Settings are simpler: your name is no longer requested or shown in greetings and the Pro card.
- Illustrated icons keep their colors and detail in transparent widgets and on Apple Watch.

## Fixes

- Improved the reliability of habit creation, widget summaries, and Apple Watch progress.
