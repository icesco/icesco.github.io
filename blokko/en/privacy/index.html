Blokko Privacy

Last updated: September 5, 2026.

Blokko is an ad-free puzzle game with no advertising SDKs or third-party analytics. It does not require a Blokko account or tracking permission.

Data stored on your device

Your game, score, coins, challenge progress and preferences are stored locally. Delivered transaction identifiers are stored with the balance to prevent duplicate credits. They are not sent to the developer’s servers.

In-app purchases

Apple handles payment and purchase confirmation through the App Store and StoreKit. Blokko receives the transaction information needed to verify and deliver coins, but never receives payment card details. Coins are stored on the device, do not expire and are not synchronized across devices. Deleting the app or its data may cause the local balance to be lost.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, the game sends achievement progress to Apple. Sign-in and the Game Center profile are managed by Apple under its terms and privacy policy. You can use the game without Game Center.

Support

If you voluntarily contact the developer, the information in your message is used to answer your request. Do not include passwords or payment details.

Managing your data

You can delete local data by removing the app and its data from your device. Information managed by Apple, including App Store transactions and Game Center, remains subject to Apple’s settings and policies.

Contact
