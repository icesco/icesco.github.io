Monstera screenshots include reference photographs from Wikimedia Commons. The photos are resized and cropped to fit; these adaptations retain their respective original licenses. The licenses cover the photographs, not the app code.

- **Ficus lyrata**: Forest & Kim Starr — [Starr 031108-0130 Ficus lyrata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Starr_031108-0130_Ficus_lyrata.jpg), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
- **Epipremnum aureum**: Mokkie — [Money Plant (Epipremnum aureum) 4](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Money_Plant_(Epipremnum_aureum)_4.jpg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Other plants may use icons. Credits for downloaded reference images are available in **Settings → Image cache → Image credits**.

Plant recognition uses the [Pl@ntNet API](https://my.plantnet.org/). Reference images in recognition results include their author and license.
